#### WR-757. A conic-pair round classified at a node of the pair takes the first plane, whichever conic its edge follows
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a round that builds is refused, status 1001; which one depends on the frame)
- **Area:** Fillets (exact round classes, `fixes.fillet_exact_round_classes`)
- **Location:** the fork's `sm_PairPlane` (tangency-labs/smlib@1b5f1e7). Rust: `crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_exact.rs`, `pair_plane`, read by `classify_blend_spine` at the centre `classify_exact_blend` is given (the trace's first point).
- **What happens:** two cylinders or cones touching a common ball the same way round meet in two plane conics; the round follows the one of the plane that holds the classifying centre. Both planes pass through the line where the conics cross, and a centre on it (a node of the pair) lies on both: the first plane tried is taken. Where that is the other conic's, the rational round is built round the wrong ellipse (its trace's ends at one parameter, a full turn), its rails come back on themselves, the rail classification reads a rollover from a point far off, and the fillet fails with status 1001.
- **Trigger:** SolidCraft's Knoten_laminiert (DC2018.3), Fusion's Fillet2: four half-tubes r 11.5 along the diagonals cross at the origin; each concave valley between two arms runs from the crossing's top (0, 0, 11.5), where two arms' offsets meet in the ellipses of the planes x = 0 and y = 0. Each valley's round is classified at that node. Three valleys took their own plane, the one toward -y took y = 0 and failed.
- **Fix:** where both planes hold the centre, the plane is the one the filleted edge leaves least at its point nearest the centre (the edge lies in its round's plane). The edge's direction is read with the class's inputs (`ClassInputs::along`, kept in the exact round's record), so a round's class read again from its supports takes the same plane.
- **Confirmed:** `tests/fillet_conic_pair_at_node.rs`, on the body as SolidCraft wrote it (`tests/data/solidcraft_knoten_laminiert_fillet2.smb`): each valley adds 2 faces and 5.1874 mm3 (Fusion's). The four valleys in one call still fail at the node, where their rounds meet with no width (a separate defect).
