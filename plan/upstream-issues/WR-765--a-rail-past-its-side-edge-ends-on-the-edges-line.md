#### WR-765. A rail running past its side edge's far end ends on that edge's line, not on the face it runs onto
- **Severity:** Wrong result (SMLib's check refuses the fillet: Bad Vertex Gap; SolidCraft's Knoten_laminiert Fillet4)
- **Area:** Fillet (Nx1 corner end: rail/side-edge vertex, end rib)
- **Location:** `SmFilletVertex::CalcRailIntEdgeuse` and `SmFilletNx1Corner::MakeCornerTopology`'s end rib. Rust: `blend/fillet_geom/p2.rs` (`calc_rail_int_edgeuse_with_solvers`), `blend/fillet_corner/p3.rs` (`adjust_rolled_rail_ends`), `blend/fillet_geom/p1.rs` (`calc_fillet_int_edgeuse_near`), `blend/fillet_geom/p5.rs` (`calc_fillet_int_side_face`).
- **What happens:** a round whose rail (r·tan(θ/2) along a side face) is longer than the side edge it ends on runs past the edge's far vertex onto the next face. On the base surface continued (`fillet_rail_end_on_continued_surface`) the rail meets the side edge's line past its end, and the end rib is cut against the end face from rail end to rail end, though its last part lies over the next face. The merge then keeps the fillet's end vertex (its zone tolerance covers the next face's point), off the next face by more than XSectTol3d (Knoten: 2.09e-5 against 2e-5; Fusion puts it on that face, 0.50002 for 0.5).
- **Fix:** the rail found past the side edge's far end continues onto the next edge at that vertex on its face, within that edge; the corner's end rib is then split at the end face's edge with the next face (a vertex at the fillet surface's crossing of that edge), one part on the end face, one on the next face, each cut against its own face.
- **Confirmed:** `tests/fillet_rail_end_past_side_edge.rs` (an L block, its valley rounded r 2, the plate's corner rounded R 8.01: the rail ends on the r 2 round, 2.5e-5 above the plate); Knoten_laminiert Fillet4's vertices as Fusion's.
