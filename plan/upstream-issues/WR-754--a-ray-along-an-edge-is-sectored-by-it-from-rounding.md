#### WR-754. A classifying ray that runs along an edge's line is sectored by that edge from rounding, and a union drops a disjoint body
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a body lost from a union, silently)
- **Area:** Classification (ray fire at a vertex), reached through the Boolean merge
- **Location:** `SmEdge::RadialSectorOfCurveClassify`, its test that the curve's direction projected square to the edge vanishes. Rust: `crates/solidcore/src/query/edge.rs`, `radial_sector_of_curve_classify`; reached from `local_curve_sector` (`crates/solidcore/src/topology/brep/p2.rs`), which classifies a ray's hit on a vertex by a vote over the radial sectors of the edges there.
- **What happens:** the test compares the projection of the curve's tangent, not unitised, with `SM_EFF_ZERO`: for a ray segment of length `L` it refuses only when `L sin(angle) < 1e-12`. A ray that runs along an edge's line to within the edge's tolerance, but further off than that, gets a sector read from the rounding of its direction, and votes with it. At a vertex with two edges the vote ties and the first region wins.
- **Trigger:** SolidCraft's Motortraeger (DC2018.3): the tube end `Body9 (2)` (6 faces) and the strut `Strebe v` (5 faces), 9 mm apart, made by extrusion; their union is the tube end alone. The strut's classifying point `(0.456789, 34.5, 12.5)` fires +x, 1e-11 from the line of the tube end's edge 9 (tilted by about 1e-12 rad), and hits that edge's vertex. Edge 9's edge uses vote for the tube's solid region, edge 10's for the void, and the tie puts the strut inside. In SolidCraft the six struts and tube ends of `Combine1` are united pairwise (`smlib_boolean_union_all`), so this pair loses the strut and the combine 165 mm³, with no error.
- **Fix:** the curve leaves along the edge, into no sector, also when it keeps within the edge's tolerance of the edge's line over its length (`sin(angle) * length <= tolerance`); that edge use is refused as for a vanishing direction, and the vertex's other edges decide.
- **Confirmed:** `query::edge::tests::a_union_keeps_a_body_whose_ray_runs_along_an_edge`, on the two bodies as SolidCraft wrote them (`tests/data/solidcraft_motortraeger_*.smb`): 6 faces before, 11 after. The C++ test is the same comparison (the Rust is ported from it); the pinned C++ was not run on these bodies.
