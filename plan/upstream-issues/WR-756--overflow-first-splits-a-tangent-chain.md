#### WR-756. Overflow-first splits a tangent chain, and each half ends at a smooth vertex no End builds
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a round that builds in one pass is refused, status 1001)
- **Area:** Fillets (the C API's overflow-first rounds)
- **Location:** the C API's `overflow_first` (`smlib_ops.cpp`, the fork's 9ba2b12). Rust: `crates/solidcore-ops/src/blend.rs`, `overflow_first`; SolidCraft's copy in its `crates/smlib/src/core/blend.rs`.
- **What happens:** when blends of several edges overflow, each asked edge is classified alone and those that overflow alone go first, the others after on what is left. Mitred pairs go together; edges of one tangent chain do not. A chain member that goes first ends, in that pass, at the smooth vertex where it runs on into a member left for later, and an End at a smooth vertex whose rail rolls over is refused ("a rail rolling over into an end is not built").
- **Trigger:** SolidCraft's Knoten_laminiert (DC2018.3), Fillet2: a tube end's arc on a 0.5 mm plate rounded r 2 with its tangent chain, the arc, its two runs on over the tube's root round (Fillet1) and two plate-top stubs on to the plate's corners. Together the session builds it (-33.238 mm3, 5 faces); with overflow first the stubs overflow alone and go first apart from the chain, and the call fails with status 1001.
- **Fix:** a round running on tangentially into another asked round (the kernel's `tangent_chain`, at the executive's default tangency, 10 degrees) goes first with it, as a mitred one does.
- **Confirmed:** `crates/solidcore-ops/tests/blend_overflow_first.rs`, `a_tangent_chain_goes_first_as_one_blend`, on the body as SolidCraft wrote it (`crates/solidcore/tests/data/solidcraft_knoten_laminiert_fillet2.smb`): 30 faces and 33.238161 mm3 less, the kernel's one-pass build. The C API shares the defect (the port is the C API's).
