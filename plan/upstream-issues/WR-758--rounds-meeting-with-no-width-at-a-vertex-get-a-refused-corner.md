#### WR-758. Several rounds that all lose their width at one vertex get a corner patch that is refused
- **Severity:** Wrong result (rounds that build one by one are refused together, status 1001)
- **Area:** Fillets (corner classification, `SmFilletCorner::Create`)
- **Location:** `SmFilletCorner::Create`: one round ending at a vertex where its side faces are tangent is a degenerate corner (`CheckNx1Degeneracy`, `SmFilletDegenerateCorner`), the round ending in the point; two or more rounds at such a vertex are classified by their count (Nx2, 4x3, ...) and get a corner patch. Rust: `crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_corner/p3.rs`, `FilletCorner::create_with_solver_lookup`; `crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_corner/topology/end_corners.rs`, `make_degenerate_corner_topology`.
- **What happens:** where every round at the vertex has lost its width there, the rounds meet in the vertex only and there is no gap to patch; the Nx2 corner finds no topology ("Unable to Make Topology"), the 4x3 corner no geometry, and the fillet fails.
- **Trigger:** SolidCraft's Knoten_laminiert (DC2018.3), Fusion's Fillet2: four half-tubes r 11.5 along the diagonals cross at the origin, their faces all tangent at the crossing's top (0, 0, 11.5), where the four concave valleys between them start. Each valley rounds r 2 alone (+2 faces, +5.1874 mm3, with WR-757), and two opposite ones together; two adjacent ones fail at the top.
- **Fix:** where every round at the vertex is degenerate there (`check_nx1_degeneracy`), the corner is one degenerate corner ending them all at a single fillet vertex on the corner vertex, each round's rails dropped there as for one.
- **Confirmed:** `tests/fillet_conic_pair_at_node.rs`, `the_four_valleys_round_as_fusion_s_fillet2`, on the body as SolidCraft wrote it (`tests/data/solidcraft_knoten_laminiert_fillet2.smb`): the four valleys in one call, 33 faces and 4 x 5.1874 mm3 more, Fusion's Fillet2.
