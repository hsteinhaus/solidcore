#### WR-761. The offset of a surface's regular pole is extrapolated from step-off points, so it is no point and its side is not singular
- **Severity:** Wrong result (an offset face's pole not one point; its side not found singular; a shell around it fails)
- **Area:** Surfaces (offset surface evaluation), reached through shells and offsets
- **Location:** `SmOffsetSurface::EvaluatePlainOrSimple`: where a base derivative vanishes (a pole), the offset's point and derivatives are extrapolated linearly from two step-off evaluations. Rust: `crates/solidcore/src/surface/offset_surface.rs`, `OffsetSurface::plain_or_simple`.
- **What happens:** at a regular pole (one tangent plane) the offset of the pole is one point, the pole moved along its normal, and the offset's derivative along the collapsed side is zero. The extrapolation gives neither exactly: the offset's pole points spread (by 1e-6 on a fillet's conic-pair round, 4e-6 off the exact point), and the side's derivative is off zero (2.6e-5), so `GetSingularities` finds no pole on the offset. A Simple evaluation took the general path there, with no pole handling at all.
- **Trigger:** SolidCraft's Knoten_laminiert (DC2018.3), Shell1 after Fillet2: the four valley rounds end in a pole at the crossing tubes' node. Their offsets' rails ended at the extrapolated pole, 4e-6 off where the offset arms' edges end, and the shell's merge related edges inconsistently there (status 1001).
- **Fix:** at a pole of the base, in both evaluations, the offset's point is the pole moved by the distance along the base's own normal there (`EvaluateNormal`, its limit at the pole), and its derivative along the collapsed side is the distance times the normal's change along the side (a central difference): zero at a regular pole, the true value at a cone's apex, whose offset side is a circle. The derivatives across the side keep the extrapolation; the never-zero first derivative of a full evaluation stays.
- **Confirmed:** `surface::offset_surface::tests::a_regular_poles_offset_is_one_point`: a sphere's offset by +-2 is one point at its pole, at the pole times 7/5 and 3/5, and its pole side is singular (it was not). On Knoten every offset rail ends at the exact node.
