#### WR-755. An Nx1 end face between two cliff side edges is the rail-1 side's, so a round and its mirror image end differently
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a round leaves the body open, or ends on a Coons patch where the twin ends on its face)
- **Area:** Fillets (Nx1 mixed-convexity corner topology)
- **Location:** `SmFilletNx1Corner::MakeCornerTopology`, the multi-neighbour end face with more than three edges at the vertex, where neither side edge has the filleted edge's convexity ("both are cliff edges, just pick one"). Rust: `crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_corner/topology/nx1_end.rs`, `FilletCorner::finish_nx1_end`.
- **What happens:** the end face to extend is always the first side edge's face, the one on the rail-1 side. After the topology the corner checks that the extended end surface holds the other side edge (`SimpleCoincidenceChecker`) and, when it does not, drops the extended-surface cap for a Coons patch (`m_bExtendedSurfacePatch` false). Which side is rail 1 follows the handedness of the faces, so a mirror image picks the other face: where one side's face, extended, holds the other side edge and the other's does not, the round on the body gets its cap and the round on its mirror image the patch. With `fixes.fillet_patch_keeps_fillet_edges` the stitch then measures the fillet's end edge against the patch, whose domain does not reach it (a 2.35 mm gap from a projection clamped to the patch's boundary), and the body comes out open.
- **Trigger:** SolidCraft's Motortraeger (DC2018.3): a round of 3 on the concave edge between the wall `y = 36` and a strut face, `z` 0 to 11. The strut at `x = 19` is modelled, the one at `x = 1` its mirror image about `x = 10`. At `(19, 36, 0)` the first side is the strut's sloped face, which extended holds the other side edge (`y = 36, z = 0`): +20.827 mm3, one face. At `(1, 36, 0)` the first side is the bottom face `z = 0`, which does not hold the other side edge (the strut's sloped edge): SolidCraft's fillet fails, "SMLib's result is not a closed solid". Fusion rounds both.
- **Fix:** between two cliff side edges the end face is the side whose extended surface holds the other side edge (the check the corner makes after), the first side when neither or both do.
- **Confirmed:** `tests/fillet_mirrored_concave_edge.rs`, on the body as SolidCraft wrote it (`tests/data/solidcraft_motortraeger_mirrored_fillet.smb`): both rounds close, add one face and 20.827 mm3, equal to 1e-9. The pinned C++ was not run on this body; the port's pick is the C++'s.
