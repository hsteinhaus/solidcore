#### WR-753. `SmLine` mirrors as the B-spline of its whole analytic domain, so a mirrored body's lines are good to 1e-8 and crossings on them are lost
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a boolean fails, or misses a crossing, on a mirrored body)
- **Area:** Curves (mirror), reached through `SmBrep::Mirror`
- **Location:** `SmLine` inherits `SmBSplineCurve::CreateMirrorCurve`, which mirrors the curve's NURB and returns it as a plain B-spline. Rust: `crates/solidcore/src/curve/curve/p2.rs`, `Curve::create_mirror_curve` (the `Line` arm), `crates/solidcore/src/curve/line.rs`.
- **What happens:** an unbounded line's NURB spans its whole analytic domain, `[-SM_INFINITE_PARAMETER, SM_INFINITE_PARAMETER]`, so its two control points lie 1234567 line lengths out. `SmBrep::Mirror` replaces every edge line with the mirror of that NURB. The mirrored edge is no longer a line (no analytic intersections) and every point on it is a difference of coordinates near 5e7: good to some 5e-9 where the line was exact. `sm_FilterSolution` keeps an interior curve/curve crossing only when its gap is below the scaled zero (5e-11 here), so the crossing Newton finds on such an edge (gap 1.6e-9) is thrown away as not exact.
- **Trigger:** SolidCraft's Knoten_laminiert (DC2018.3): a 0.5 mm plate made by extruding a sketch, mirrored, then united with a half-cylinder whose end face is coplanar with one side face of the plate. Classifying the cylinder face's quarter-ellipse edge on the plate's face misses its crossing with the plate's top edge (`(16.248, 0, 0.5)`, ellipse 0.0305, line 0.6437), so the whole edge is classified in the 0.5 mm face; the point solve of its far end, 11 mm off that face, finds nothing and the union fails with status 1001 (`PointClassification::compute_point_parameters`). The plate's unmirrored twin, whose edges are still lines, unites with the half-cylinder. The same edge trimmed to its edge interval gives the crossing.
- **Fix:** `Line::create_mirror_curve`: a line mirrors to a line, its point and direction mirrored, its domain, scale and parameterisation kept, its NURB mirrored with it. It evaluates as exactly as the line it came from.
- **Confirmed:** `topology::brep::mirror::tests::a_mirrored_box_keeps_exact_line_edges` (a box mirrored about a slanted plane keeps line edges meeting their vertices to 1e-12; an unbounded line mirrored is a line exact to 1e-12, 5.4e-9 off before the fix). The C++ shares the defect: `SmLine` has no `CreateMirrorCurve` of its own, and the port's `Line` arm is the inherited B-spline override.
