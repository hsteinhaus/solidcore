#### WR-759. A rational patch's pole is snapped to its side's mean, not the frame's origin, so its offset is refused as folding with a wall of 0
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a shell of a regular face refused, "the largest wall that works is 0")
- **Area:** Offset (shell construction S1, the regularity certificate; `fixes.offset_construction_*`, solidcore-native, #2350)
- **Location:** `crates/solidcore/src/offset/construction/sheets.rs`, `regular_poles`: a patch side that collapses to one point (a pole) is moved into the pole's tangent frame and set to the side's mean.
- **What happens:** the frame's origin is the pole, but the side's mean after the move is that origin only to round-off (1e-16). A polynomial side is then still constant, so its derivatives along the side vanish exactly; a rational side's homogeneous points are the pole times weights that differ, so they do not vanish, and the forms `X^2`, `q0`, `q1` are no longer zero along the side (1e-61 instead). The side's common factor is not found (`side_order` 0), the pole's limit is not divided out, and the subdivision walks into the pole's corner, where `Q` is 0 to round-off (-3e-63): the face is reported folding, with a largest wall of 0.
- **Trigger:** SolidCraft's Knoten_laminiert (DC2018.3), Shell1 (wall 0.5 inward) after Fillet2: each of the four valley rounds is a rational conic-pair net whose end at the crossing tubes' node is a pole (its row's weights 2.11, 1.06, 0.53, the pole (0, 0, 11.5)); all four were refused.
- **Fix:** in the frame, the pole is the origin exactly.
- **Confirmed:** `offset::construction::sheets::tests::weighted_domed_quarter_with_a_pole_certifies`: the domed quarter with weights `0.5^j` along v (the same dome) certifies a 1 mm wall either way; it was refused as folding at 49.5. The four Knoten rounds certify. The shell then fails further on (the offset faces' UV trim curves at the offset pole), a separate defect.
