#### WR-762. An offset face whose loop runs out and back along one curve (a slit) is merged as it is
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a shell refused, status 1001, with SolidCraft's fixes)
- **Area:** Offset (the extended offset's faces before `PiecewiseMerge`)
- **Location:** `SmOffsetExecutive::DoExtendedOffset`: each offset face's loop is the original face's loop with every edge replaced by its offset; nothing splits a face whose region comes apart. Rust: `crates/solidcore/src/offset/offset_executive/p2.rs`, `do_extended_offset`, before `piecewise_merge`.
- **What happens:** where a wall is as thick as a side is high (a 0.5 plate shelled 0.5 inward, its bottom open), the side's offset keeps no height but at its ends: its loop runs out along a curve and back along the same curve between coincident vertices (a slit), or back along part of an edge. The merge mates both of the slit's edges to the one edge they make (`TopologyIntersector::SetRelationships`, whose `Relate` status upstream ignores and the fork's `brep_edit_statuses_pass_on` passes on: status 1001; without it, a later `make_faceuse` failure on SolidCraft's Knoten_laminiert).
- **Fix:** before the merge, an offset face whose loop holds a slit is replaced by the faces of its loop's parts either side of it (`remove_slits`): two edges of one loop running out and back between the same vertices, or vertices that coincide as `DoStitching` glues them, whose curves coincide as `DoStitching` finds edges to glue (`Stitch::edges_coincide`, its comparison extracted unchanged into `Stitch::edge_pair_gap`); an edge of the loop run back along part of another is first split at the loop's vertex lying on it, as `DoStitching` splits edges at vertices. The parts are rebuilt on the face's surface by the crossing-loop resolution's tail (`rebuild_from_stretches`), the new faces taking the old one's attributes and offset-map places. A face with no slit is left as it is.
- **Confirmed:** `tests/shell_wall_as_thick_as_a_plate.rs`: a 20 x 20 x 0.5 plate with a 10 x 10 x 10 tower, bottom open, shelled 0.5 inward: 390, 16 faces (refused before); with the plate's corner cut at 45 degrees 387.75, 17 faces; a 1.0 plate as before, 409.5, 21 faces. SolidCraft's Knoten_laminiert Shell1 then gets past the merge.
