#### WR-760. An offset face put on its extended surface drops its UV trim curves anew, and at a pole the drop goes astray
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a shell refused, status 1001)
- **Area:** Offset (the extended offset's `ExtendResultFaces`)
- **Location:** `SmOffsetExecutive::ExtendResultFaces`: each offset face is put on its extended surface (`ReplaceSurface(.., FALSE)`, which removes the face's UV trim curves) and its trim domain is read off UV trim curves dropped anew (`CalculateUVDomainFromUVTrimCurves`, which creates them). Rust: `crates/solidcore/src/offset/offset_executive/p2.rs`, `extend_result_faces_prefix`.
- **What happens:** the face already has its UV trim curves on the offset surface, and the extension is that surface over its domain, so they are its curves there too. Dropped anew, an edge curve that starts at a pole of the surface (where every parameter of the collapsed side is the same point, and the extension's side need not be singular) gets an arbitrary start parameter: the curve dog-legs out of the domain, the trim domain runs past the extension's, and the face is refused.
- **Trigger:** SolidCraft's Knoten_laminiert (DC2018.3), Shell1 after Fillet2: the valley rounds' offsets; their rails start at the offset of the rounds' pole at the crossing's node, and their dropped curves started at v -81.8 and -32.9 on a v range of 0..2.
- **Fix:** the face's UV trim curves are kept and given back after the surface is replaced, where every one of them lies in the replaced surface's domain and the extension and the replaced surface agree at its points (sampled) within the face's tolerance; otherwise they are dropped anew, as upstream.
- **Confirmed:** `tests/shell_knoten_laminiert.rs` (the body as SolidCraft wrote it): Shell1 gets past the extended offset (its merge then fails at the node, a separate defect).
